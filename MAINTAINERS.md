## Who decides

The owner decides the project's scope, its releases and everything on the ask-first list: the
definitions of severity, category, feature area and tie kind; every rule's severity; the score, the
plan and the options catalogue; the public interface; the scanner's allow-lists; production
dependencies; and the project's guidance and CI.

## How rules and severities are accepted

- A rule is accepted when it cites a public source from the accepted hosts with the date read, fires on
  the demo project, has a near-miss that does not fire, and states facts only.
- A rule's severity is the owner's call. The portability proof on stock PostgreSQL is evidence for it,
  not a substitute.
- A factual error in a rule is a bug and is corrected in a patch release.
- No entry is added to the options catalogue at a vendor's request outside its published criteria.
